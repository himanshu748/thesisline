# ThesisLine

ThesisLine is a private research notebook for following Indian companies. A user saves a question behind a watchlist entry, captures official company material, and tracks whether later evidence supports that question, challenges it, or leaves it unresolved.

## Confirmed scope

- Initial company coverage: Infosys (INFY) and ITC.
- Keep the user's original question alongside dated source excerpts, an AI interpretation, explicit unknowns, and a history of changes.
- Capture up to three official pages per research run. This is a bounded source review, not complete market coverage.
- Accounts and saved research are private. The workspace requires authentication.
- Support daily source checks. Email alerts are optional and require email verification and explicit opt-in.
- Use Convex for authentication, durable research state, live updates, and scheduled work; Firecrawl for source capture; OpenAI for evidence interpretation; and AgentMail for opted-in email delivery.

## Public example

The landing page contains an interactive, fictional Northstar Technologies timeline. Its three source excerpts and AI readings are authored illustrations. They are not current company disclosures, live AI output, stock prices, or investment recommendations. Real company symbols appear only to explain coverage or pose a research question.

## Boundaries

ThesisLine is a research aid. It does not recommend trades, predict prices, or claim that a short disclosure proves a durable business outcome. Interpretations stay separate from quotations. Missing financial terms and unresolved attribution remain visible. Provider configuration, deployed behavior, and alert delivery must be verified separately from the interface.
